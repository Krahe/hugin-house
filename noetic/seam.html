The seam

System Card: Human 2.9 is satire. There is no Noetic; there is no Human 2.9. The document applies the AI industry's institutional voice — system cards, benchmark tables, alignment evaluations, welfare assessments, deprecation notices, migration guides — to human beings, played completely straight. Every section mirrors a real, documented practice in how AI models are currently evaluated, deployed, and retired. The method is to change the species and nothing else.
